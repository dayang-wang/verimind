How it works

From submission to evidence of learning.

A simple workflow that fits after an assignment—not another exam students have to prepare for.

01 / SUBMIT

Use the work already completed

The student or instructor uploads a PDF assignment, report, or project. VeriMind uses that submission as the only source for the check.

02 / VERIFY

Ask five focused questions

AI identifies the work’s most important ideas and creates a brief check that probes recall, interpretation, and application.

03 / REVIEW

Give instructors useful signals

Responses, confidence, and source references help the instructor review understanding. VeriMind supports judgment—it does not replace it.

“The question is not whether AI touched the work. The question is whether the student understands it.”
VeriMind assessment principle
✓

Ground every question in the submission

No unrelated trivia and no guessing what was taught outside the submitted work.

✓

Keep the experience lightweight

A focused 3–5 minute check produces useful evidence without becoming an additional assignment.

✓

Make uncertainty visible

Confidence ratings can distinguish secure understanding from a lucky answer.

✓

Keep educators in control

Results inform an instructor’s review. Final academic decisions remain with the professor.
